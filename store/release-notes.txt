First release.

Your real steps carry a wanderer across Elsewhere, a hand-drawn continent of 3,500 kilometres. Eleven
regions, 109 named places, forty one encounters with people who remember you, and a road that ends.

1,300 steps make a kilometre, at most 40 kilometres a day. Camp is painted where your feet stopped and
the journal writes the day down for you.

No account, no ads, no purchases, no location and no internet permission.
